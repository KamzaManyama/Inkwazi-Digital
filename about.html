01Who we are

Practical support for business owners and entrepreneurs.

Inkwazi Digital is a business compliance consultancy based at 504 Magalela Drive, Tweefontein A, Phumula, KwaMhlanga, South Africa. We support business owners and entrepreneurs with practical assistance across key compliance areas, including CIPC, SARS, labour-related matters and the Central Supplier Database (CSD).

Alongside compliance support, we design and develop websites and digital tools that help businesses work more efficiently. Our aim is to make important business processes easier to understand and manage, so clients can focus more of their time on operating and growing their businesses.
