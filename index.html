01Who we are

Practical support for business owners and entrepreneurs.

Inkwazi Digital is a business compliance consultancy based in KwaMhlanga, South Africa. We support business owners and entrepreneurs with practical assistance across key compliance areas, including CIPC, SARS, labour-related matters and the Central Supplier Database (CSD).

02What we do

Business compliance support

01

CIPC Support

Assistance with company-related matters, including registration and company record or annual-return processes, where required.

02

SARS Support

Guidance and assistance with tax-related administration and compliance processes, based on the client's circumstances.

03

Labour-Related Matters

Practical support with applicable employment and labour administration requirements.

04

Central Supplier Database (CSD)

Assistance with supplier profile registration, updates and related CSD administration.

05

Additional Business Compliance

Help identifying relevant administrative requirements and the next steps for a business's particular needs.

06

Business Readiness

Support to help business owners organise key business information and work through relevant compliance tasks.
